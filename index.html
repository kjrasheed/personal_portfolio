CASE_01

Substance Use and Property Crime

State-Level Multiple Regression · 2018

Combined CORGIS substance-use data with FBI property-crime rates to test relationships across U.S. states. The workflow included exploratory analysis, a nine-variable saturated model, backward elimination, multicollinearity review, and residual diagnostics.

Final model
Cocaine use among ages 18–25 and 26+
Model fit
R² = 0.427
Diagnostics
Shapiro-Wilk p = 0.2581
